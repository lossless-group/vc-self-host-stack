## Why Care?

A share link is the one URL that leaves the building. It goes to a founder, an
LP, someone who has never heard of us — and until today, clicking one flipped the
address bar to `outline-production-3d8d.up.railway.app`. The pretty path worked,
right up until the moment it mattered.

Now every wiki lives at a name we own and **generates clean links itself**:

```
wiki.palmer-ai.lossless.at
wiki.reach-edu.lossless.at
wiki.the-water-foundation.lossless.at
```

Not rewritten after the fact — *born* correct, because each Outline's `URL` env
var points at its own host.

## What's New?

- **Three custom-domain cutovers** — Railway domain + Vercel DNS (CNAME +
  `_railway-verify` TXT) + `URL` env var + `CONFIG_EPOCH` bump, per client. Certs
  issued in minutes; verified by `curl -v` showing
  `subject: CN=wiki.<client>.lossless.at`.
- **[[Homebase-MCP-One-Connector-Per-Client]]** — the spec for the aggregating
  MCP. One connector per client fronting CRM, wiki, data room, and post planner,
  with our agent-skills served as prompts.
- **[[vc-firm-profile-ingest]]** — an agent-skill that takes *"create a profile
  for Kalos Ventures"* and produces a Twenty company record, an attached research
  note, and a cross-linked Outline page, with no copy-paste between systems.
- **[[Which-Domain-Hosts-What-lossless-at-vs-didi-sh]]** — filed as an issue, not
  a decision, because nothing was decided.

## The thing a redirect can never do

Worth stating plainly, because it has now bitten twice in two different shapes.

**A redirect points at a host. It cannot hide one.** `lossless.at/<client>/wiki`
307s to wherever the app lives, and the browser lands there — address bar and
all. That is fine for a dashboard you log into and useless for a link you send to
someone else.

Masking it under the path was not available either: Outline assumes it lives at a
host **root** and requests `/static` and `/api` as absolute paths. Proxied under
`/palmer-ai/wiki/`, those resolve against `lossless.at` and belong to no client.
Same family as Postiz's cookie-domain constraint — **some apps will not live
under a subpath, and no amount of URL cleverness changes that.**

So the fix had to be at the source: give the app a real host, and tell the app
that is its address. The portal path stays the memorable front door and redirects
there; each Railway domain stays attached, so every link already sent still
resolves.

## The spec, and the gate that turned out to cover half of it

[[Per-Client-Stack-Deployment-Spec-Twenty-First]] Phase 6 has blocked homebase on
the id-didi-sh spec absorbing *"one-service-or-two, workspace scoping, BYO-key
custody."* All three are **secrets-and-identity** questions. An aggregator that
federates tools a client already pays us to run, and serves our own skills,
answers none of them. Two things were fused under one word and the gate froze
both.

The load-bearing proposal is **H2: use each client's own Twenty as the OAuth
authorization server.** Every client user already has a Twenty account, and
Twenty already ships a working authorization server. That buys per-user identity
and per-client scoping with zero new identity infrastructure — and makes homebase
a *consumer* of the id-didi-sh decision rather than an input to it. It is also
the riskiest assumption in the document, so Phase 0 does nothing but try to kill
it across all four harnesses before anything real gets built.

## The Skills plane already works, crudely

Before building anything, the Skills plane was proven end-to-end with no new
service: `vc-firm-profile-ingest` was published into Palmer AI's Outline as an
**Agent Playbooks** document, then enumerated and read back over MCP from a
different client.

That experiment settled a real design question. Outline exposes both
`list_templates` (returns every body **inline**) and `list_collection_documents`
(titles and ids, then `fetch` per doc). Templates look like the closer analogue to
MCP prompts, but they invert the cost model — at ten playbooks, asking "what's
available here?" would load ten full bodies. **So homebase's `prompts/list` must
return names and descriptions only**, with bodies materialized on `prompts/get`.

It also exposed the obvious flaw in the interim: **publishing forks the source.**
The Outline copy is what agents load, the git copy is what we edit, nothing
reconciles them. Until homebase serves `context-v/agent-skills/` directly the
discipline is *edit in git, re-publish to Outline* — and it argues the spec's
open question #4 toward fetch-at-runtime over bake-at-build.

## Gotchas

1. **The Vercel CLI defaults to the wrong scope.** `vercel dns add` returned
   `permission_denied` for `lossless.at` because the CLI was scoped to
   `mpstatons-projects`; the zone lives under **`colearn-labs`**. Pass
   `--scope colearn-labs` or nothing works, with an error that sounds like an
   access problem rather than a scope one.
2. **Batched `vercel dns add` calls silently drop.** Four records issued in one
   shell produced one record. Issued individually, all four landed. Verify with
   `vercel dns ls` rather than trusting the exit path.
3. **Changing Outline's `URL` forces a fresh sign-in** — the session cookie is
   host-scoped. Harmless now that magic links work (2026-08-08); it would have
   been a lockout a day earlier.
4. **Outline's frontmatter renders badly in Outline.** Publishing a `SKILL.md`
   verbatim leaves a mangled `## name: … description: …` heading, since Outline
   stores the title separately. Strip the YAML; keep the trigger phrasing as a
   blockquote.

## Left open, deliberately

The CRMs and the data room are still on Railway hostnames. Different situation
from the wikis: Twenty's `SERVER_URL` is also its **OAuth issuer**, baked into
the MCP connector's discovery documents, so changing it forces every connected
Claude Desktop to re-authenticate. Nobody passes links out of those the way they
do from a wiki — share links were the actual problem, and share links are fixed.

Reach's Postiz stays on `postiz.reach-edu.didi.sh`. It is the only working Postiz
in the stack, and it works *because* of the cookie host a move would change.
