## Why Care?

`context-v/` is where this repo keeps what we know about running other
people's tools: which domain hosts what, why Postiz costs so much, what
Railway does before you can configure a service, and where the last session
left off. Until today an agent had to open each file to learn what kind of
doc it was. Now both of the repo's `context-v/` folders are Open Knowledge
Format (OKF) v0.2 bundles, so any OKF reader can list what's here and what
each doc is for without opening anything.

## What's New?

- **All 24 docs in `context-v/` declare their `type`**, the Train-Case name
  of the folder they sit in: `Explorations` (8), `Reminders` (4),
  `Handoffs` (3), `Issues` (3), `Specs` (3), `Plans` (2), `Decisions` (1).
- **`context-v/index.md`** marks the bundle with `okf_version: "0.2"` and
  lists its seven folders.
- **Seven folder indexes**, one line per doc with its lede (the one decision
  has no lede, so its line is the title alone).
- **`hubs/event-hog-mcp/context-v/` is its own bundle**, with a root index
  and a `decisions/` index. Its one decision already declared its type.

## Under the Hood

- Part of the tree-wide OKF sweep; see the Context Vigilance Kit's plan,
  [[Retrofit-Every-Repo-to-OKF-Then-the-Kit-Frontmatter]].
- Every edit was made by hand, one file at a time. The doc diff is 24 added
  `type:` lines and nothing removed. No dates moved: this is a compliance
  change, not a content change.
- `context-v/agent-skills/` sits beside the bundle, not in it: skills follow
  the Agent Skills spec. The upstream apps under `core/` and the
  `hubs/lossless-at` submodule are other repos and weren't touched.
