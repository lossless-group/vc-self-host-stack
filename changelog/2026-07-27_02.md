## Why Care?

The pitch behind self-host-stack is that a client's whole tool suite — CRM,
wiki, docs, email — can live in ONE Railway project they own, each tool a
self-contained bundle with its own datastores. Until today that was a
one-bundle story (Twenty CRM). Docmost makes it two: palmer-ai now has a
wiki/documentation surface next to its CRM, same project, same conventions,
zero new SaaS subscriptions.

It's also the first non-Twenty bundle to go through the per-client deploy
discipline, which is the real test of whether the conventions generalize —
they did, and the deploy took one pass.

## Under the Hood

Docmost is architecturally *simpler* than Twenty on Railway, and the deltas
are worth noting for the next bundle:

- **No S3 needed.** Twenty forced bucket storage because its server and
  worker share a filesystem Railway can't give them. Docmost is one service,
  so upstream's local-volume default (`/app/data/storage`) just works.
- **Postgres 18 mounts at `/var/lib/postgresql`** — not the `/data` suffix —
  because the pg18 image keeps PGDATA in a versioned subdir; no PGDATA
  override, no initdb complaints.
- **The lossless-run delta paid off immediately:** image services deploy at
  creation before variables exist, so the set-vars-then-redeploy sequence
  from that delta log was followed from the start. App healthy ~30 seconds
  after redeploy, no failed deployments at all this time.
